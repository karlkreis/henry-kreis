The design

A personification in miniature

A right-facing female personification fills the oval field. The word AMERICA follows the upper edge, while a copyright symbol followed by HK anchors the composition at lower right.

Broad, simplified planes and sharply defined contours give the small pin the presence of a larger sculptural relief.

Family oral history also recalls two gold examples and holds that one was stolen from Henry Kreis's widow. This account has not been independently documented.

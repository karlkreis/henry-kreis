Stone · Bronze · Relief

Artist & craftsman

“Materials, technique, planes, textures, and curves mattered deeply—but they remained means of communicating emotion.”

Trained through apprenticeship in Germany and shaped by years in the studios of major American sculptors, Kreis built a career that crossed the traditional boundaries between fine art and applied art.
